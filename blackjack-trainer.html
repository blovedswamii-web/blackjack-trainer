Index plays (Hi-Lo true count)

Illustrious 18 and Fab 4 (Schlesinger). At or above the index, make the deviation; below it, play basic strategy. Values are for shoe games and adjust for H17 where it matters.

Bet ramp

Edge ≈ edge off the top + 0.5% per true count. Kelly bet = bankroll × fraction × edge ÷ 1.3 (the variance of one hand). Rounded down to table-minimum units and capped at the table maximum.
